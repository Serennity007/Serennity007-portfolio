# Portfolio direction

Audience: research collaborators and engineering recruiters. Preserve the established geek style; use actual research and reproducible projects as the subject.

Palette: deep ink #050b12, ice #e8f2f5, cyan #70e5ea, muted blue #adc2cf, existing theme-aware surface and line tokens. Noto Sans SC carries Chinese content; JetBrains Mono is limited to code, project identifiers and the opening monogram.

Layout: identity and research on the left, runnable workbench on the right. Below research, give the quant toolchain a full-width feature and use four compact rows for supporting projects. On mobile, stack in reading order. In the interactive CV, research and projects have direct links and quieter editorial entries. Standard Chinese/English printable sources and PDF downloads remain unchanged.

The memorable moment is a finite 6-second personal introduction. Light converges on the name, then reveals research, established achievements and three current projects in one continuous composition. The identity is the focal point; facts stay readable together, rather than disappearing between slides. The exit begins under the homepage entrance to preserve continuity.

The introduction uses supplied public facts: Liang Zhengtao; a BIT master's student; agent collaboration, agent safety and multimodal LLM safety; a paper accepted at KSEM (CCF-C); first in the Guizhou University cohort (1/69); Huawei ICT National First Prize. Project names are the quant toolchain, GhostTrack and XSS Audit Desktop. It adds no new publication, award, performance or security-detection claims.

Motion thesis: one authored Experience surface; layered linework gathers around the identity, research and evidence enter in order, and the composition opens into the existing hero. Use a finite SVG/GSAP sequence, scoped transforms/opacity and a clipped name reveal. No media downloads, sound, fake loading percentage, scroll lock, indefinite loader or new runtime dependency. Limit the sequence to three geometry groups and short text staggers; do not animate width/height or run idle frame loops. Test at 320 px, the desktop sidebar and full desktop sizes.

Session revisits and deep links bypass autoplay; the explicit replay button is beside the main hero actions. Escape, skip, wheel, pagehide, backgrounding, print and reduced-motion changes terminate the sequence. The global motion toggle also controls it. A new session key allows a visitor to see the revised introduction once. A 7.5s JS watchdog and 7.9s CSS escape prevent blocking content if GSAP fails.

GSAP is vendored with its license header; transforms and opacity are animated through a labeled timeline. Normal content is visible without JavaScript or GSAP. Pointer sheen/tilt is scoped to the workbench and quant feature; long CV text stays flat. The existing demand-driven pointer controller is retained.
